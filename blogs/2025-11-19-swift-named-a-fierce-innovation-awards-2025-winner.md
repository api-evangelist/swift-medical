Swift Medical was named a 2025 Fierce Innovation Award winner. Toronto, ON, November 19 — Swift Medical, the global leader in AI-powered wound care solutions, has been named a 2025 winner of the prestigious Fierce Innovation Awards for its Swift Skin & Wound™ platform. The program showcases outstanding innovation that is driving improvements and transforming the industry while demonstrating a proven solution to improve patient engagement, drive significant efficiencies and revolutionize the healthcare industry.
