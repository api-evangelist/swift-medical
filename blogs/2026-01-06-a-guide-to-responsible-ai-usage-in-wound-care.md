The adoption of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in healthcare has rapidly accelerated, bringing with it both unprecedented opportunities and significant challenges. For wound care specifically, AI promises to revolutionize everything from assessment accuracy to prior authorizations. To ensure its safe and responsible deployment, The US Joint Commission and the Coalition for Health AI (CHAI) have released crucial guidance.
