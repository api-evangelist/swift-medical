Clinical trials for chronic wounds have long struggled to efficiently collect consistent wound metrics and compare subjective assessments that can vary across clinicians and sites. The Wound Care Collaborative Community (WCCC) is advocating for a modernized approach. They are seeking a future of skin and wound clinical trials that embraces objective tools and richer data.
