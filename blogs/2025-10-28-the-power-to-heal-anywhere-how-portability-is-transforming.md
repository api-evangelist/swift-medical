Over the past decade, wound care has undergone a remarkable transformation. Advances in AI, imaging, and connectivity—accelerated by the pandemic—have redefined how clinicians deliver care and how patients receive it. At the center of this change is portability: the ability to bring advanced wound care directly to the patient, wherever they are.
